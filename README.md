# EFDSOE · European Forum for Digital Sovereignty & Open Source Development

Official GitHub space for **EFDSOE** (AdminEFDSOSD) — advancing open standards, Public Code, and independent European digital infrastructures across policy, public administration, academia, and civil society.

### Focus Areas
- **Digital Sovereignty & EuroStack:** Federated cloud, open protocols, and interoperable European software ecosystems.
- **Public Money, Public Code:** Reference architectures, procurement guidelines, and open-source blueprints for public institutions.
- **Sovereign AI & Open Data:** Transparent, auditable, and European-hosted AI frameworks.
- **Academic & Policy Transfer:** Joint working groups, symposia, and practice labs in cooperation with our foundation.
